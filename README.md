# Financial Transformation & Predictive Analytics

**An end‑to‑end financial analytics project using Excel and Power BI to move a growing UK healthcare provider from reactive expense tracking to proactive, scenario‑based financial planning.**

## 📖 Overview

A multidisciplinary private healthcare provider based in Leeds, UK, experienced rapid clinical growth but faced significant financial instability. With over 60% of monthly costs tied to fixed expenses and inconsistent insurance‑driven revenue, leadership lacked the forecasting tools needed to manage cashflow and control operating expenses. This project delivers a structured financial planning framework—combining Excel modeling with Power BI dashboards—to enable predictive budgeting, scenario simulation, and real‑time financial visibility.

The solution empowers NovaCare to:
- **Forecast revenue and expenses** across a rolling 12‑month horizon
- **Monitor cashflow daily** and anticipate liquidity shortfalls
- **Analyze variances** between actuals and budget in real time
- **Simulate best‑, base‑, and worst‑case scenarios** based on patient volume, pricing, and cost inflation
- **Make informed, data‑driven decisions** that protect margins and support growth

## Business Challenge

Despite strong clinical performance (92% patient satisfaction, 35% reduction in external lab outsourcing), NovaCare struggled with financial volatility:

- **Cashflow Instability** – Delayed insurance reimbursements caused unpredictable liquidity gaps
- **Rising OPEX** – Operating expenses climbing 6–9% annually without corresponding controls
- **Reactive Decision‑Making** – No structured forecasting meant leadership could not anticipate financial stress or plan for investment needs
- **Fixed Cost Burden** – Salaries, rent, and medical supplies consumed a disproportionate share of monthly revenue

The executive team needed a financial planning system that could turn historical data into forward‑looking insights and enable “what‑if” analysis.

## ✨ Key Deliverables

- **12‑Month Budget Model** – Excel‑based budget linking historical trends, patient volume projections, pricing assumptions, and cost drivers to produce a line‑item monthly forecast.
- **Cashflow Forecasting Tool** – A 13‑week rolling cashflow model that accounts for insurance payment cycles, supplier terms, and payroll timing, with daily and weekly views.
- **Variance Analysis Engine** – Automatically compares actual financial results against the budget, flagging deviations >5% and providing root‑cause drill‑down by department and expense category.
- **Scenario Simulation Module** – Models three macro scenarios (Best, Base, Worst) by adjusting patient volume, pricing per visit, and inflation rate inputs, showing impact on profitability and cash reserves.
- **Power BI Financial Dashboard** – Interactive dashboard displaying key financial KPIs: revenue, OPEX, EBITDA margin, cash balance, budget variance, and scenario projections with month‑to‑date and year‑to‑date filters.
